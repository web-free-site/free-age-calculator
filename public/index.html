    Frequently asked questions

    Is Free Age Calculator free to use?

    Yes. All calculations run in your browser with no account and no paid API.

    How is exact age calculated?

    The tool counts full years, then months, then days between birth date and a target date, borrowing days from the previous month when needed (including leap years).

    Can I compare age between two dates?

    Yes. Use “Compare with another date” to see exact age on any past or future day.

    Does school enrollment mode use official government rules?

    No. You enter your own cutoff date and the tool shows exact age on that day. Admission rules vary by school and region—always confirm with the institution.

    Is my date of birth sent to a server?

    No. Dates are processed locally. Optional browser localStorage may remember your birth date on this device only.
