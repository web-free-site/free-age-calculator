How to calculate age for school enrollment

Schools often ask whether a child was a certain age on a cutoff date (for example, the first day of term or a fixed date in the autumn). This site does not encode any country’s legal rules—it only computes exact calendar age on the date you enter.

What you need

  1. Child’s date of birth
  2. Cutoff date published by your school or district (enter it exactly as stated)

Use the calculator

On the home page, enter the birth date, then fill in School enrollment cutoff. You will see years, months, and days on that cutoff.

Important disclaimer

Enrollment policies differ worldwide and can change yearly. Minimum ages, grade placement, and exceptions are set by local authorities—not by this calculator. Always verify requirements with the school before enrolling.
