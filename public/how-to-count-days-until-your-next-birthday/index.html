How to count the days until your next birthday

Counting down to a birthday sounds simple, but months have different lengths and some people are born on 29 February. This guide shows how to work out the months and days left by hand, and how to check the answer in a few seconds with the Free Age Calculator.

Step 1: Find the date of your next birthday

Put your birth month and day in the current year. If that date is still ahead (or is today), that is your next birthday. If it has already passed, move it to next year. Example: born on 14 March, today is 22 November → the next birthday is 14 March of next year.

Step 2: Count the whole months

Move forward one calendar month at a time while you stay on or before the birthday. From 22 November you can step to 22 December, 22 January and 22 February — that is 3 whole months. One more step (22 March) would go past 14 March, so stop.

Step 3: Count the remaining days

Count the days from the last step (22 February) to the birthday (14 March). February has 28 days in a common year, so that is 6 days to the end of February plus 14 days in March = 20 days. In a leap year February has 29 days, giving 21 days. The answer is therefore “3 months and 20 days” (or 21 in a leap year).

If you want the total number of days

Some people prefer a single number (“112 days to go”). Add the days left in each month: the rest of the current month, every full month in between, then the birthday’s day number. This is where mistakes creep in, because you must use the correct length for every month, including February.

Birthdays on 29 February

In years without a 29 February there is no exact anniversary. People and institutions use different conventions—often 28 February or 1 March. A countdown tool has to pick one, so if the exact date matters (for example for a legal age), check the rule that applies to you.

Do it instantly in your browser

On the home page, enter your date of birth. The Next Birthday In card shows the months and days left, the date of your next birthday, and a live countdown. Everything is calculated locally in your browser; your date of birth is not sent to a server.
